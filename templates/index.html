DIAGNOSTIC FOUNDATION

Bridging Radiological Triage with Transfer Learning

Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC) accounts for over 90% of kidney malignancies. In early asymptomatic stages, lesions are frequently identified incidentally during cross-sectional abdominal CT imaging. Prompt categorization of renal masses is critical for staging, surgical planning, and survival outcomes.

This platform operationalizes an automated computer vision pipeline trained to assist clinical decision support. By transferring feature extractors from deep convolutional layers, subtle differences in parenchymal attenuation, vascular invasion, and necrosis patterns are evaluated with rigorous repeatability.

01

VGG16 Block 5 Fine-Tuning

Blocks 1–4 remain frozen to preserve foundational edge/texture filters, while Block 5 conv weights adapt directly to renal parenchymal variations.

02

Rigorous Data Distribution Alignment

Eliminated the input-domain discrepancy by aligning training, validation, and inference pipelines under uniform ImageNet channel mean subtraction.

Preprocessing Resolution

Previous iterations dividing raw pixels by 255 produced severe numerical mismatch against VGG16's ImageNet weights. Replacing that with preprocess_input centers channels around [-103.9, -116.8, -123.7], eliminating saturation and stabilizing gradients across both classes.
