About the Project

Production-grade ML pipeline for medical imaging

This project implements a full MLOps workflow for kidney CT scan classification. Starting from raw image data, it covers ingestion, model preparation, training, evaluation, and serving — all tracked and reproducible.

The model uses a pre-trained VGG16 backbone with ImageNet weights, with controlled fine-tuning of the final convolutional block (block5) for domain adaptation to medical images.

Understanding Kidney Disease

This system classifies kidney CT scans into two categories, assisting radiologists with preliminary screening.

✅

Normal Kidney

A healthy kidney showing normal cortex, medulla, and collecting system without any visible mass. CT appearance is uniform and well-defined.

⚠️

Kidney Tumor

Abnormal growth in the kidney parenchyma. Renal cell carcinoma (RCC) is the most common type. CT reveals hypervascular lesions with irregular borders.

🔬

CT Scan Imaging

Computed Tomography provides cross-sectional views of kidney anatomy. Contrast-enhanced CT is the gold standard for detecting renal masses.

🤖

AI-Assisted Screening

VGG16 detects subtle texture patterns in CT slices correlated with malignancy, acting as a first-pass screening tool to support clinical decisions.
